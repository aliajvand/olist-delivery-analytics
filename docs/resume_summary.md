# Resume Bullet Point & Impact Summary

**E-Commerce Fulfillment SLA Intelligence & Fixed-Capacity Expedite Engine**

### One-Sentence Elevator Summary
Engineered an institutional fulfillment risk and dispatch optimization platform evaluated on 100K+ e-commerce orders, strictly enforcing 39 purchase-time features to prevent carrier scan leakage and delivering a 2.07x lift via a fixed-capacity expedite policy deployed with FastAPI and Plotly/Dash.

### Resume Bullet Points (Accomplished [X] measured by [Y] by doing [Z])

- **Lead Predictive Modeling & Leakage Auditing:** Engineered a late delivery risk classifier across 100K+ Brazilian e-commerce transactions, strictly enforcing a 39 purchase-time feature horizon to eliminate post-checkout carrier tracking scan leakage under chronological validation.
- **Operations Policy & Capacity Optimization:** Formulated a capacity-constrained expedite dispatch policy allocating the top 5% highest-risk orders to priority shipping, achieving a 2.07x lift over random triage while balancing a 5.4:1 asymmetric loss ratio (65 BRL penalty vs 12 BRL expedite cost).
- **Transit Lead Time Regression:** Built a gradient boosted transit duration regressor achieving a 4.09-day MAE, translating spatial geodesic distance and package dimensions into reliable customer delivery promise estimates.
- **Full-Stack Microservice Deployment:** Architected a modular FastAPI backend serving real-time risk scores and corridor analytics, coupled with an interactive Plotly/Dash decision cockpit and a relational Star schema analytical SQL layer.
